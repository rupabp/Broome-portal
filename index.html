M-CHAT-R/F · 18 months
Autism Screening — 18-Month Well Visit
AAP-required at every 18-month visit · 20 yes/no items · ~2–5 min · Ages 16–30 months · Includes Follow-Up interview for positive screens (score 3+)
Follow-Up (M-CHAT-R/F): Required if initial score is 3–7. Administer only the follow-up questions for failed items. If score remains 2+ after follow-up → refer for evaluation. Score 8–20 → bypass follow-up and refer immediately. The follow-up interview is included in the PDF above.
M-CHAT-R/F · 24 months
Autism Screening — 24-Month Well Visit
AAP-required at every 24-month visit · 20 yes/no items · ~2–5 min · Also used for children who scored low risk at 18 months and need rescreening at 24 months
Follow-Up (M-CHAT-R/F): Required if initial score is 3–7. Score 0–2 after follow-up → screen negative (rescreen at future visits if concerns). Score 2+ after follow-up → refer for diagnostic evaluation and early intervention. Score 8–20 → refer immediately without follow-up.
